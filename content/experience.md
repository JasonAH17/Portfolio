## Climate Change Education Volunteer, BINUS University with Teach For Indonesia (TFI), at SD Negeri 23 Palmerah

**Duration:** May 2025 (the session was on Friday, 9 May 2025)

**Photos:** /images/experience/climate-class.jpg (shown first), /images/experience/climate-team.jpg (behind it, opens in the viewer)

Taught a fifth-grade class at SD Negeri 23 Palmerah, West Jakarta, about climate change as part of a Character Building Pancasila project on SDG 13 (Climate Action). Our team ran an interactive session on what climate change is, how it affects daily life, and simple things the students can do for the environment, then observed the class and interviewed students and teachers for the project article.
